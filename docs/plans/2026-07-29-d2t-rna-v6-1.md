A hash chain alone does not prove chronology, external pre-registration, or the absence of a privately recomputed alternative chain. Such claims require an append-only or external anchor and are not made by Task 1.

The hash-only truth fields are not treated as cryptographic hiding for low-entropy ON/OFF or directional content. Task 3 must combine sealed access control and semantic sanitization with a registered reveal/commitment construction before any blinding claim is made.
